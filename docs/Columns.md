# Columns

Paint-ready decorative columns built over a Sonotube concrete-form tube, with a wire/stretch-wrap/tape/paper shell.

## Layer stack

From the form outward:

1. **Sonotube** — the round cardboard concrete-form tube, used as the reusable mold/core.
2. **Wire** — 1/2" hardware cloth wrapped around the tube, closed with hog rings, for rigidity and texture.
3. **Stretch wrap** — heavy-duty shrink/stretch film over the wire, smooths the surface and acts as a release layer.
4. **Carpet tape** — double-sided tape over the stretch wrap, gives the paper layer something to grip.
5. **Paper** — kraft butcher paper pressed onto the tape, forms the paintable outer shell.
6. **Paint** — tempera paint applied last with the paint brushes.
