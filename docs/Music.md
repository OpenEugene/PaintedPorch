# Music

Music is what turns an empty lot or open street into a place people actually want to be. A set draws people in off the sidewalk and gives them a reason to linger — to stop, talk, and look at what's around them instead of walking past it. That's the same spirit behind the Painted Porch itself: open to the street, not tucked away behind an invitation.

This page credits the musicians who play at Painted Porch events, starting with the first.

## Artists

### Free Static

Free Static is a modular synth duo — Courtney Stubbert and Chris Ruiz. They're the headliner at the [Sept 5, 2026 Art Hop event](Home.md#visit) at Westish Shop (see [Events](Events.md) for details). Listen to their album [Shark's Lung in Plastic](https://freestatic.bandcamp.com/album/sharks-lung-in-plastic) on Bandcamp.
